One company's bad day doesn't have to be yours

Big outages happen to big companies — Microsoft, Google, and everyone else included. None of them are immune, and neither is any business that puts everything behind a single provider. Atelier is built so that when something else goes down, your quotes and invoices don't go down with it.

Independent infrastructure, on purpose

Atelier runs on Google Cloud. That's a deliberate, separate choice from the infrastructure behind tools like Microsoft 365 or Azure. If Microsoft has an outage, it has no bearing on Atelier. If Google Cloud ever has an issue, your Microsoft-based tools carry on unaffected. Two independent providers means there's no single shared point of failure between the two — your quoting and invoicing isn't riding on the same infrastructure as the rest of your stack.

This isn't an argument against using Microsoft, Xero, or anything else you already rely on. Use what works for your business. Atelier simply isn't betting your ability to quote and invoice on any one of them staying up.

Built to keep working, connection or not

Atelier is installable as an app on desktop, Android and iOS. Once it's loaded your catalog, customers, and recent quotes and invoices, that data stays available even if your connection drops. Anything you create or update while offline — a new quote, a status change — queues up and sends the moment you're back online, without you having to do anything extra.

Use both. That's not a problem.

Plenty of businesses run more than one system at once, and there's nothing wrong with that. Keep Microsoft, Xero, Zoho or whatever else is already working for you — Atelier isn't asking you to rip anything out. It's there as a second, independent place where your quoting and invoicing keeps moving, no matter what else is happening elsewhere.
