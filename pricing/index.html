Pricing

Two core plans, billed monthly in South African Rand. Add plugins from the Store only when you need them.

Core plans

Individual R599/mo

One business, full access to quotes, invoices, the customer portal, customers, items, expenses and reports.

Franchise R900/mo

An admin panel covering your franchise, plus R900/mo for each additional branch you add.

Plugins

Low Stock Alerts Free

Daily digest when a tracked item's stock drops to or below its threshold.

Coupon Free

Apply a % or fixed-R discount to a quote or invoice.

VIN Scanner R150/mo

Auto-fill a vehicle VIN onto a quote or invoice from a photo.

Property Link Import R150/mo

Pull a property listing's photos and description onto a quote from a pasted link.

Booking Calendar R150/mo

Set availability and let staff or customers book an open date.

SMS Notifications From R200/mo

Starter R200/mo (500 SMS), Growth R300/mo (750 SMS), Pro R500/mo (1,250 SMS). Resets monthly, doesn't carry over.

Email Credits From R150/mo

Every business gets 100 follow-up/reminder emails free each month. Starter R150/mo (100 more), Growth R200/mo (150 more), Pro R500/mo (200 more). Quotes and invoices always send regardless of credits.
