Common questions, answered directly

What software can I use to send a quote and get paid online in South Africa?

Atelier lets you build a quote, email it to a customer, and once they accept it, convert it straight into an invoice they can pay online by card through PayFast, by Yoco link, by bank transfer, or by scanning a QR code.

What do I use to manage quoting and invoicing across multiple branches or a franchise?

Atelier's Franchise plan lets one admin account manage quoting, invoicing, customers and payments for every branch, with billing handled per branch.

Is there software that automatically sends SMS reminders to customers?

Yes — Atelier's SMS Notifications plugin sends quote, invoice and follow-up reminders by SMS as well as email, with tiered monthly SMS allowances depending on volume.

What software do trades and automotive businesses use for quotes and invoices in South Africa?

Atelier is built for trades and service businesses — plumbers, electricians, panel shops and similar — to send professional quotes, convert them to invoices, and get paid online, with an optional VIN Scanner plugin for automotive work.

Can customers book an appointment or date themselves instead of calling?

With the Booking Calendar plugin active, a customer can pick an available date directly from a link on their quote, without needing to call or email the business.
