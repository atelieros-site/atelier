What's included

Quotes & invoices

Build a quote, send it by email, and convert it to an invoice in one click. Customers can accept or decline a quote from an online link, no account needed.

Online payments

Invoices can be paid by card through PayFast, by Yoco link, by bank transfer or by scanning a QR code — the customer portal shows whichever your business has set up.

Customers, items & expenses

Keep a customer list, track inventory items with low-stock alerts, log expenses, and run reports across all of it.

Franchise & branch management

Run more than one branch or franchise location from a single admin account, with billing handled per branch.

Plugins that grow with your business

Add only what you need, when you need it, from the in-app Store.

Low Stock Alerts Free

A daily digest whenever a tracked item's stock drops to or below the threshold you set.

Coupon Free

Create a coupon and apply a % or fixed-R discount to any quote or invoice with one click.

VIN Scanner R150/mo

Point a camera at a vehicle's VIN plate and auto-fill it on a quote or invoice line.

Property Link Import R150/mo

Paste a listing link from Property24, Private Property, RE/MAX, Pam Golding, booking.com and more, and pull its photos and description onto a quote.

SMS Notifications From R200/mo

Send quote, invoice and reminder notifications by SMS as well as email.

Email Credits From R150/mo

Extra follow-up and reminder email volume beyond the free monthly allowance — quotes and invoices always send regardless of credits.

Booking Calendar R150/mo

Set your working days and closed dates, then let staff or customers book an available date straight from a quote link.
