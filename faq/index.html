Frequently asked questions

What is Atelier?

Atelier is a Business OS for South African small and medium businesses. It combines quoting, invoicing, a customer portal with online payments, customer and inventory management, expense tracking, reports, and multi-branch/franchise administration in one platform.

How much does Atelier cost?

The Individual plan is R599/mo for one business. The Franchise plan is R900/mo for the admin panel, plus R900/mo for each additional branch. Optional plugins range from free to R500/mo depending on the plugin and tier.

Can my customers pay invoices online?

Yes. Invoices include a customer portal link where a customer can pay by card through PayFast, by a Yoco link, by bank transfer, or by scanning a QR code, depending on what the business has configured.

Does Atelier support multiple branches or franchises?

Yes. The Franchise plan lets a business manage multiple branches from a single admin account, with billing handled per branch.

What plugins are available for Atelier?

Low Stock Alerts and Coupon are free. Paid plugins include VIN Scanner (R150/mo), Property Link Import (R150/mo), Booking Calendar (R150/mo), SMS Notifications (from R200/mo, tiered by SMS volume), and Email Credits (from R150/mo, tiered by extra email allowance).

Do quotes and invoices stop sending if I run out of email credits?

No. Quotes and invoices always send regardless of your Email Credits balance. Only optional follow-up and reminder emails are limited by the monthly email allowance; every business gets 50 of these free each month before needing to top up.

Can customers book an appointment or date through Atelier?

With the Booking Calendar plugin active, a business sets its working days and closed dates, and staff can book directly from the dashboard, or customers can pick an available date themselves from a link on their quote.

Who is Atelier built for?

Atelier is built for South African small and medium businesses that send quotes and invoices regularly, such as trades, service businesses, automotive shops, and franchises with multiple branches.
