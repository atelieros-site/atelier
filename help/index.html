How it works

Atelier follows the natural shape of getting paid for work: build a catalog of what you sell, quote a customer, get it accepted, convert it to an invoice, and get paid.

  1. Build your catalog. Add items (with stock on hand) or services (with optional estimated hours) once — reuse them on every quote and invoice from then on.
  2. Send a quote. Pick items and services from your catalog or add custom lines, and email it to your customer.
  3. Customer accepts. They review and accept the quote from their own link — no account needed on their end.
  4. Convert to invoice. One click turns the accepted quote into an invoice, carrying every line item across.
  5. Get paid. The customer pays by card (PayFast), Yoco link, bank transfer, or by scanning a QR code. Stock on hand updates automatically once an invoice is marked paid.

Quotes & Invoices

Quotes and invoices share the same line-item builder. Pull items straight from your catalog (pricing and descriptions fill in automatically) or add one-off custom lines for anything not in your catalog.

Once a quote is accepted, convert it to an invoice in one step — nothing needs to be re-typed. Invoices include a customer-facing payment link, and you can track each one's status (unpaid, partially paid, paid) from your dashboard.

Reminders can go out automatically as a quote or invoice approaches its due date, by email and, with the SMS Notifications plugin, by SMS too.

Dashboard & overview

Your overview screen gives you a snapshot of the business at a glance: what's been paid this month, what's still outstanding, and how many quotes have gone out — plus a running view of recent invoices and their status.

If you're on the Franchise plan, an admin dashboard rolls this up across every branch, so you can see the whole operation from one account.

Notifications (like a low-stock alert or a newly accepted quote) show up via the bell icon, so you don't have to go looking for them.

Plugins

Plugins let you add exactly what your business needs, without carrying the cost or clutter of features you don't use:

  • Low Stock Alerts — get flagged when a catalog item's stock on hand drops below a threshold you set.
  • Coupon — run discount codes on quotes and invoices.
  • VIN Scanner — scan a vehicle's VIN to pull its details straight into a quote (built for automotive and panel shops).
  • Property Link Import — pull in property listing details for businesses dealing in listings.
  • Booking Calendar — let customers pick an available date themselves from a link on their quote.
  • SMS Notifications — send quote, invoice, and reminder messages by SMS as well as email.
  • Email Credits — extra monthly allowance for reminder and follow-up emails beyond the free tier.

More plugins are added over time as new trades and workflows come on board. Have an idea? Tell us what you'd like to see.

Documents

Every business gets a Documents vault for company-wide files — policies, certificates, anything you want on hand. Find it under My Documents in the sidebar.

Customers and quotes get their own documents too. Click the paperclip icon on a customer or quote to attach a file directly to that record — it's automatically filed under that customer's Documents tab, with an inline preview so you don't have to download it just to check it.

AI Task Tracker

The AI Task Tracker lives under Overview — click the dropdown arrow next to Overview in the sidebar to expand it. It's a running list of follow-ups: call this customer, chase that payment, don't forget to message someone about a quote.

Add a task manually from the tracker itself, or quick-add one straight from a customer or quote page so it stays linked back to that record, with an icon for the channel (call, WhatsApp, email, SMS, meeting) and a short note on why. Tasks can also be flagged as AI-suggested once a business connects its own AI provider. Every task has its own notes thread, so anyone following up can see who did what.

AI Assistant

Want the Task Tracker to start suggesting follow-ups on its own? Connect your own AI provider — Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or Grok — under Settings > AI Assistant.

Your API key is stored securely in its own isolated secret for your business, never shared across tenants, and you can remove it at any time.
